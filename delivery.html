Customer Information

Delivery

We want your First Team Footballer game to reach you safely and as quickly as possible.

UK Standard Delivery

UK standard delivery is £4.95 per order.

Dispatch

Orders are normally dispatched within 2–4 working days.

Delivery Service

We currently use Royal Mail Tracked 48 for UK orders. Royal Mail aims to deliver within 2–3 working days after dispatch.

Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates and may occasionally vary due to circumstances outside our control, including delays within the courier network.

Tracking

Where tracking information is available, it will be provided once your order has been dispatched.

Problems With Your Delivery

If your order has not arrived within a reasonable time after dispatch, please contact us and we will help investigate the delivery.

For delivery questions, contact info@firstteamfootballer.com .
