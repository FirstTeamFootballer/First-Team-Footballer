Customer Information

Returns

We hope you enjoy First Team Footballer, but if you need to return your order, please contact us and we will help you through the process.

Changing Your Mind

If you purchased your game online, you may cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it. Once you have told us that you wish to cancel, the item should normally be returned within a further 14 days.

How To Request A Return

Please email info@firstteamfootballer.com before returning your order. Include your name, order details and the reason for contacting us so that we can provide the appropriate return instructions.

Condition Of Returned Items

If you change your mind, please return the game in its original condition, with all game pieces, cards, instructions and other components included. Please also return the original packaging where reasonably possible. You may open the product to inspect it, but if it has been used, damaged, or handled beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it, we may make an appropriate deduction from the refund to reflect any reduction in value.

Return Postage

Unless the product is faulty, damaged or not as described, customers are normally responsible for the cost of returning the item.

Refunds

Where a refund is due, we will process it using the original payment method. Refunds will be handled within the time required by applicable UK consumer law.

Faulty Or Damaged Products

If your game arrives faulty, damaged, incomplete or not as described, please contact us as soon as possible so that we can arrange an appropriate remedy in line with your statutory rights.

To request a return or report a problem, contact info@firstteamfootballer.com .
