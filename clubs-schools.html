Learning Through Football

Turn Their Love Of Football Into An Opportunity To Learn.

For many young people, football is something they connect with immediately. First Team Footballer uses that passion to encourage learning through reading, discussion, decision-making, problem-solving, communication and play.

Read & Understand

Reading questions, interpreting information and thinking about what is being asked.

Think & Reason

Considering possibilities, making decisions and explaining why.

Speak & Listen

Discussing ideas, expressing answers and listening to other viewpoints.

Communicate

Building confidence in expressing thoughts, opinions and football knowledge.

Work Together

Turn-taking, teamwork, respect and positive competition with others.

Develop The Person

Confidence, resilience, leadership and learning how to handle success and setbacks.

For Clubs & Academies

Take Development Beyond The Training Pitch.

Training time is precious. First Team Footballer gives coaches another environment to explore areas of player development that cannot always be covered in depth during a practical session.

Technical

Understanding skills, techniques and football actions.

Tactical

Game understanding, positioning, decision-making and recognising situations.

Physical

Preparation, fitness, recovery, nutrition and looking after the body.

Psychological

Confidence, resilience, concentration, attitude and responding to setbacks.

Social

Communication, teamwork, leadership, respect and relationships.

Some Things Are Coached On The Pitch. Some Things Need To Be Talked About.
