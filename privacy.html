Customer Information

Privacy

First Team Footballer LTD respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website or purchasing from us.

Who We Are

First Team Footballer LTD is responsible for the personal information we collect and use in connection with this website and our customers.

If you have any questions about how we use your personal information, you can contact us at info@firstteamfootballer.com.

Information We May Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, telephone number, billing or delivery address, order details and communications you send to us.

Payment information is processed securely through Stripe. We do not need to receive or store your full payment card details ourselves.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information where necessary to process and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, provide customer support, manage returns or refunds, communicate with you about your purchase, prevent fraud and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Our Lawful Basis

We process personal information where it is necessary to perform our contract with you, where we have a legal obligation to do so, or where we have a legitimate business interest that does not override your rights.

Where consent is required for a particular use of your information, we will ask for it separately.

Who We Share Information With

We may share personal information with trusted service providers where this is necessary to operate our business and fulfil your order.

This may include Stripe for payment processing, delivery companies such as Royal Mail, website or hosting providers, and professional advisers or authorities where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Payments Through Stripe

Payments are processed through Stripe. Stripe may collect and process personal and transaction information necessary to complete your payment, prevent fraud and provide its payment services.

Stripe processes this information in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable data protection law.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including fulfilling orders, dealing with customer enquiries and returns, keeping appropriate business records and meeting legal, tax and accounting requirements.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted or securely disposed of where appropriate.

Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights over your personal information, including the right to request access to your data, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion or restriction of your information, object to certain uses of your information, and request transfer of your information where applicable.

If we rely on your consent for a particular use of your information, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Data Protection Complaints

If you have a concern about how we use your personal information, please contact us first at info@firstteamfootballer.com so that we can investigate the matter.

You also have the right to raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, business practices or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Privacy enquiries: info@firstteamfootballer.com
